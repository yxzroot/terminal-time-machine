# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming community for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, or background.

## Expected Behavior

- Be constructive and assume good intent.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing technical decisions and user needs.
- Protect others' privacy and do not post secrets or personal data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and disruptive bad-faith behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other action needed to protect the community.
